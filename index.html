Focus privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Focus works on your device. It has no account system, advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, or developer-operated server. The app does not send your focus history, settings, app selections, or motion readings to its developer or sell them.

What Focus stores

Focus saves completed and quit session records, including session names, dates, duration, mode, quit attempts, mini-game counts, and a summary of the number of blocked apps, categories, or sites. It also saves your theme, default duration and mode, onboarding choice, reminder preference and time, and the Screen Time selection you choose. An active session is saved so closing the app does not reset it. These records stay in the app's local database and preferences.

Screen Time and app blocking

With your permission, Apple's Family Controls picker lets you select apps, categories, and websites to block. Focus stores Apple's selection tokens and passes them to Apple's Managed Settings service to apply the block. Focus does not obtain a browsing history or a record of what you do in other apps. You can change your selection in Focus Settings or manage Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings.

Notifications and motion

Optional local notifications announce session completion and daily reminders. They are scheduled on your device, not sent through a Focus server. Some mini-games read live device tilt or orientation. Focus does not save or transmit those motion samples. The session timer reads a continuous system clock and a boot identifier solely to measure elapsed time without trusting changes to the device date. Timing information stays on the device.

Retention and your controls

Session records remain until you clear them. Clear session history removes saved completed and quit records while preserving your settings and blocked-app selection. Delete all data in Focus Settings removes session records, stored app selection, preferences, and Focus's pending and delivered notifications, then returns to onboarding. It is unavailable during an active focus session. It does not revoke permissions granted in iOS Settings or erase copies in existing device backups. Removing the app deletes its local app container; Apple controls backup retention and system permission records.

Apple services and diagnostics

Depending on your iOS settings, Apple may include app data in device backups and collect system diagnostics. Focus uses the system logging service for app state diagnostics. Delete all data does not erase Apple's backups or system-managed logs. Those services are governed by Apple's privacy settings and policies; Focus does not operate its own diagnostic collection service or cloud sync.

Changes and questions

Changes to this policy will appear here with a new effective date. For privacy questions, use the developer contact provided on Focus's App Store listing.
